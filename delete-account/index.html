How to request account deletion

You can ask us to delete your KiwiHarvest Satellite Collections account and associated data in either of these ways. You do not need the app installed to use option 2.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open the KiwiHarvest Satellite Collections app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab, then Security.
  3. Under Deactivate account, enter a reason such as "Please delete my account and data".
  4. Tap Request deactivation and confirm.

Option 2: By email

  1. Email hello@kiwiharvest.org.nz with the subject "Account deletion request".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your account, and include your full name and Satellite Partner organisation.

We may contact you to verify your identity before deleting anything. We will confirm by email once your request has been completed.

What we delete

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your name and email address
  • Your Satellite Partner organisation link and role
  • App diagnostics and crash data linked to your account, according to our service providers' standard retention

What we may keep, and for how long

Some collection records may need to be kept because they form part of KiwiHarvest's operational, donor, audit or legal records. This can include collection dates, donor sites, food weights and categories, and collection outcomes.

Where we keep these records, we remove or de-identify your name and email address wherever practicable. We keep them only for as long as reasonably required for reporting, audit, legal or contractual requirements, then securely delete or de-identify them.

More information

See our Privacy Statement for full details of the information we collect and how we use it. If you have questions, contact hello@kiwiharvest.org.nz or phone 0800 601 609.
