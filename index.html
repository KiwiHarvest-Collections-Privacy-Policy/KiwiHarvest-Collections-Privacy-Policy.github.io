Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KiwiHarvest Limited (“KiwiHarvest”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use the Satellite Collections mobile and web application and related services (the “Services”).

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand) and this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please contact your coordinator before continuing to use the Services.

Definitions

Personal information: Information about an identifiable individual, such as name, email address, phone number, organisation, and account details.

Operational data: Information about collections and related activity, such as donor sites, weights, submission notes, and outcomes you record in the app.

Satellite Partner: Your organisation, authorised to collect food on behalf of KiwiHarvest under the Satellite Partner Memorandum of Understanding.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account and contact information

When you activate or use an account, we may collect information you or your coordinator provide, including:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Partner organisation and role
  • Login credentials (stored securely; passwords are not readable by staff)

1.2 Collection and operational information

When you use the Services to manage assignments, we may collect:

  • Collection job details and donor site information assigned to you
  • Weights, categories, notes, photos (if enabled), and submission outcomes
  • No-collection reasons and related notes
  • Timestamps and status of submissions (e.g. draft, submitted, flagged)

1.3 Device and usage information

We may automatically collect limited technical information to operate and improve the Services:

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Approximate network information (e.g. IP address) for security and diagnostics
  • Basic usage events (e.g. screen errors, sync status) to keep the app reliable

1.4 Location (optional)

If you choose to open maps or share location for navigation to a donor site, location may be processed by the maps provider you select (for example Google Maps or Apple Maps). We only use location for collection-related navigation features you initiate.

2. How we use your information

We use personal information and operational data to:

  • Create and manage your collector account and permissions
  • Assign, display, and record food collections on behalf of KiwiHarvest
  • Report aggregated collection weights and outcomes to KiwiHarvest and relevant food donors
  • Communicate about your account, assignments, safety updates, and support requests
  • Improve the reliability, security, and usability of the Services
  • Meet legal, audit, and partnership obligations under the Satellite Partner MoU

3. How we share information

3.1 Within KiwiHarvest and your organisation

Collection records and account details may be visible to KiwiHarvest staff and authorised coordinators at your Satellite Partner organisation so they can support activations, reporting, and quality assurance.

3.2 Food donors and reporting

Aggregated or site-level collection data (such as weights by category) may be shared with the relevant food donor as part of KiwiHarvest’s reporting commitments. We do not sell your personal information.

3.3 Service providers

We may use trusted providers for hosting, analytics, messaging, or maps. They may process information only to provide services to us and must protect it appropriately.

3.4 Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, or where necessary to protect the safety of people, investigate misuse of the Services, or defend our legal rights.

4. Retention

We keep personal information and collection records for as long as needed to operate the Services, meet reporting and audit requirements with partners and donors, and comply with the law. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No system is completely secure; please protect your login details and tell your coordinator promptly if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as required under the Privacy Act 2020.

6. Your rights (Privacy Act 2020)

Under New Zealand privacy law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct personal information that is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity. Some collection records may need to be retained for reporting or legal reasons even if your account is closed.

7. Cookies and similar technologies (web)

If you use the web version of the Services, we may use essential cookies or similar technologies needed for login, security, and core functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may limit some features.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by email to the address on your account. Continued use of the Services after an update means the updated policy applies to that ongoing use.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or concerns about this policy:

  • KiwiHarvest Limited
  • Email: support@kh.org.nz
  • Ask your Satellite Partner coordinator to escalate to KiwiHarvest if needed

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).
