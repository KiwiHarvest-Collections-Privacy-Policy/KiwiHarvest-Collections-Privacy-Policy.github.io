1. About this Privacy Statement

KiwiHarvest Limited respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Statement explains how KiwiHarvest collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you use the KiwiHarvest Satellite Collections mobile application, web application and related services, together referred to as the Services.

It applies primarily to people using the Services on behalf of organisations participating in KiwiHarvest's Satellite Collections programme.

KiwiHarvest handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020 and the information privacy principles that apply in New Zealand.

2. Who is responsible for your information

KiwiHarvest Limited operates the Services and is responsible for personal information collected through them.

Satellite Partner organisations may also hold information about their own staff, volunteers or collectors under their own privacy arrangements.

Where a Satellite Partner provides your personal information to KiwiHarvest so that you can be invited to use the Services, KiwiHarvest will take reasonable steps to make you aware that we have received your information, why we have received it and how it will be used, unless notification is not required under the Privacy Act.

3. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the Services.

Account and contact information

We may collect your name, email address, phone number if provided, Satellite Partner organisation, role and information associated with your account and access permissions.

Login credentials are stored securely. Passwords are not readable by KiwiHarvest staff.

Collection information

When you use the Services to undertake or record food collections, we may collect information including collection assignments, donor site information, food weights and categories, collection notes, photographs where that feature is enabled, reasons why a collection did not take place, submission outcomes, timestamps and the status of submissions.

Device and usage information

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate, secure and improve the Services. This may include device type, operating system, app version, approximate network information such as an IP address and basic information about errors, synchronisation and use of the Services.

Location information

KiwiHarvest does not continuously track your location through the Services.

If you choose to open a map or use a location feature to navigate to a donor site, location information may be processed by the mapping service you choose, such as Google Maps or Apple Maps. Their own privacy terms will apply to information they collect through their services.

4. How we collect your information

We may collect information directly from you when you activate or use an account, enter information into the Services, contact KiwiHarvest or request support.

We may also receive information about you from an authorised coordinator at your Satellite Partner organisation. For example, your coordinator may provide your name and email address so that KiwiHarvest can invite you to activate an account.

Some information may be collected automatically when you use the Services, such as limited device and technical information.

We will only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary for a lawful purpose connected with the Services or KiwiHarvest's activities.

Where information is required to create or operate an account, we will make this clear where practicable. If required information is not provided, you may not be able to use the relevant part of the Services.

5. How we use your information

We may use personal information and information recorded through the Services to create and administer accounts, manage user permissions, assign and record food collections, communicate with you about collections, accounts, safety matters and support, maintain the security and reliability of the Services and meet our reporting, audit, legal and partnership obligations.

We may also use collection information to understand and report on food rescue activity, improve the Satellite Collections programme and improve the Services.

Where possible, reporting about food rescue volumes and outcomes will use aggregated information rather than information identifying an individual.

We will take reasonable steps to ensure personal information is accurate and appropriate before using it.

6. Who we may share information with

We only share personal information where there is an appropriate reason for doing so.

KiwiHarvest and Satellite Partners

Information relevant to your account and collection activity may be accessible to authorised KiwiHarvest staff and authorised coordinators within your Satellite Partner organisation where this is necessary to manage accounts, support collections, undertake reporting or provide quality assurance.

Food donors

KiwiHarvest may provide food donors with collection information relating to their sites, including information such as quantities, categories and collection outcomes.

Where possible, this information will be aggregated or provided without information that personally identifies individual collectors.

Service providers

KiwiHarvest may use trusted organisations to provide services such as technology hosting, communications, analytics, security, technical support and mapping.

Those providers may process information on KiwiHarvest's behalf only for the services they provide to us and are expected to protect that information appropriately.

Legal and safety requirements

We may disclose personal information where required or permitted by law, or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the safety of a person, investigate suspected misuse of the Services, protect KiwiHarvest's legal rights or respond to a lawful request.

KiwiHarvest does not sell your personal information.

7. Information processed outside New Zealand

Some technology or service providers used in connection with the Services may process or store information outside New Zealand.

Where KiwiHarvest discloses personal information to an organisation outside New Zealand, we will comply with the requirements of the Privacy Act relating to overseas disclosure, including requirements for appropriate privacy safeguards where they apply.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purposes for which it was collected or to meet operational, reporting, audit, legal, contractual or cultural requirements.

Closing your account does not necessarily mean that all records connected with that account will immediately be deleted. Some collection records may need to be retained because they form part of KiwiHarvest's operational, donor, audit or legal records.

When personal information is no longer required, KiwiHarvest will take reasonable steps to securely delete it, de identify it or otherwise dispose of it appropriately.

9. How we protect your information

KiwiHarvest takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

Access to information within the Services is restricted according to user roles and operational requirements.

No digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You can help protect your information by keeping your login details confidential and notifying your Satellite Partner coordinator or KiwiHarvest promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without authority.

10. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to ask KiwiHarvest for access to personal information we hold about you and to ask us to correct information that you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

Some account information may also be managed by your Satellite Partner coordinator. If information in your profile appears incorrect, you may contact your coordinator or KiwiHarvest.

11. Privacy breaches

If a privacy breach occurs, KiwiHarvest will assess and respond to it in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020.

Where a breach has caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm, KiwiHarvest will notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals as required by law.

12. Cookies and similar technologies

The web version of the Services may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for functions such as login, security and operation of the site.

Your browser may allow you to control or disable cookies. Disabling necessary cookies may prevent some parts of the Services from working correctly.

13. Changes to this Privacy Statement

We may update this Privacy Statement from time to time to reflect changes to the Services, our information practices or legal requirements.

The date at the top of this statement will show when it was last updated. Where a change is significant, we may also notify users through the Services or by email.
